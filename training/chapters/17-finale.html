    That's how our robot sees.

    Light, pixels, JPEG, USB, memory, 1,024 GPU cores, geometry, time and a lot of careful testing, every 8 milliseconds, on every camera. Now go make it better.

    Course recap
    • A global-shutter camera captures AprilTags; the Jetson decodes each JPEG and its GPU finds the tags in about 2 ms.
    • Calibration and geometry turn four corners into a robot pose, stamped with when the light was captured.
    • NetworkTables carries it to the robot, and watchdogs keep it all running on match day.
    Course recap
    • AprilTags give the robot known landmarks; a global-shutter camera with a short exposure captures them sharply.
    • Frames cross one shared USB 2.0 budget as JPEGs, get decoded on NVJPG and searched on the GPU in ~2 ms.
    • Calibration and PnP turn four corners into a pose; multi-tag and the gyro make it trustworthy.
    • Timestamps at mid-exposure, synced to the robot's clock, let the pose estimator correct the past.
    • NetworkTables carries results to the robot, and layers of watchdogs keep it all running on match day.
